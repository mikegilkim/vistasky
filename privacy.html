Information Vistasky processes

  • Google sign-in: Google verifies your account. Vistasky verifies the signed Google credential and processes its stable account identifier to keep you signed in and associate your synced preferences. Vistasky does not save your Google name or email address. Google Identity Services and Google may process information under their own terms.
  • Synced weather preferences: If you choose to sign in and save preferences, Vistasky stores the Google account identifier as a record key, saved city names and coordinates, your last selected location, temperature units, and theme in a Supabase Postgres database managed for this deployment. The deployment operator can access this data.
  • Browser storage: Vistasky does not set or read cookies. The browser stores your display theme in local storage. If you sign in, a random bearer session token is held in session storage for that browser tab and sent in authorization headers; it is cleared when you sign out or close the tab. Supabase stores only a SHA-256 hash of the token and its Google account association, with a validity period of up to 24 hours. Browser session storage is available to scripts running on this site, so keeping the app and its dependencies secure is important.
  • Weather and location requests: Searching a city sends the search text to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Forecast requests send the selected location's coordinates to Open-Meteo. If you choose device location, your browser asks permission and coordinates may be sent to Open-Meteo and BigDataCloud for forecast and reverse-geocoding responses.
  • Technical requests: The app loads interface libraries, icons, charts, and fonts from third-party CDNs and Google Fonts. Their servers may receive standard request data such as your IP address and browser details. The Google sign-in library is loaded only when you open sign-in.

Purposes and legal bases

The app processes data to provide forecasts you request, operate the optional Google sign-in and preference-sync features, remember your choices, and maintain the service. Before launch, the operator must document the appropriate GDPR legal basis for each purpose and the applicable ePrivacy rules for device storage. Device location is requested only after you choose the location feature and grant browser permission.

Cookies and third-party services

Vistasky itself does not create, read, or rely on cookies, and it does not run analytics, advertising, or personalization trackers. Google Identity Services is loaded only when you choose Google sign-in. Google may use cookies or other technologies on its own sign-in pages and services, subject to Google's privacy information and settings. Vistasky does not control Google's storage practices.

Theme and sign-in state use browser local storage and session storage, respectively. These are browser storage mechanisms, not cookies. Weather, font, icon, chart, and styling requests may still send standard network information, such as your IP address, to the named third-party providers.

Retention and deletion

On Vercel, the session's token hash is valid for up to 24 hours; expired session rows are cleaned up on the next successful sign-in. The tab's bearer token is removed when you sign out or close the tab. Synced preferences are kept in Supabase until you remove them using Delete synced weather data in the app footer. Signing out alone does not delete those preferences. The operator must document how long backup copies, if any, are retained and when deleted data is removed from them.

Your rights and international processing

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and you may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Use the in-app data deletion control for synced preferences or contact the operator using the privacy contact above. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Google, Supabase, Vercel, and other named service providers process requests needed to provide their services. Their processing locations and transfer safeguards depend on the operator's deployment and provider arrangements. The operator must identify and document applicable processors, international transfers, and safeguards before launch.
